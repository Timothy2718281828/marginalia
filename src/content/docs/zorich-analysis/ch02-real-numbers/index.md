## Chapter 2: Real Numbers

The real number system is the foundation of all analysis. Zorich builds $\R$ axiomatically: algebraic axioms, the order relation, the completeness axiom, and its consequences (nested interval theorem, Borel-Lebesgue lemma, Bolzano-Weierstrass principle). The chapter also covers cardinality — countable sets and the continuum.
